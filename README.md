## 3. Hardware LSL Data Plane vs. Dynamic Cortical Control Plane (Multi-Board uHD EEG)

Grounded in ultra-high-density EEG (uHD EEG) neuroengineering (*Lee et al., Frontiers in Neuroscience 2022* [[23]](#ref-23)):

1. **Immortal Hardware LSL Streams (Data Plane):**
   Each physical `FreeEEG16-alpha2` board (26 mm diameter, 16 channels, 120 bipolar dipoles) is bound to a **permanent hardware LSL stream** named `FreeEEG_<ID>` (e.g., `FreeEEG_4A2B`). LSL outlets are **never** destroyed, renamed, or restarted when a board is moved across the head or reassigned to another user.
2. **Out-of-Band Cortical Routing & Auto-Return (Control Plane):**
   User identity (`User1..N`), cortical zone (`Fpz`, `AFz`, `F3`, `F4`, `FCz`, `Pz`, `Cz`, `Oz`, or `UNASSIGNED`), and PGA gain (`4x..32x`) are managed out-of-band via the Web/IPC Control Plane (`router.device_mappings`) and persisted in browser `localStorage`. If a board is rebooted or temporarily loses BLE connection, the browser automatically reconnects and restores its exact cortical assignment into the same LSL stream.
3. **Multi-Board uHD Spatial Fusion on a Single 10–20 Zone:**
   Because a standard 10–20 region (such as `FCz` or `F3`) spans 60–80 mm while a `FreeEEG16-alpha2` puck is only 26 mm wide, **multiple boards (2, 3, or more) can be placed side-by-side within the same 10–20 zone** (e.g. `FreeEEG_4A2B -> FCz`, `FreeEEG_9C1D -> FCz`, `FreeEEG_7E0F -> FCz`). `DirectGPUSpectralFrontend` automatically fuses all boards assigned to the same zone via spatial phase-coherence weighting on the GPU.

## 4. Mathematical Foundations of Phase Coherence & Cortical Heterarchy

### 4.1 Volume-Conduction-Free $ci\text{PLV} \in [-1.0, +1.0]$ (*Bruña et al., 2018*)
Following *Bruña, Maestú, & Pereda (2018)* ([DOI: 10.1088/1741-2552/aacfe4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/aacfe4) [[10]](#ref-10), Eq. 14), for all $\binom{16}{2} = 120$ unique dipole pairs per board and $32$ phase slots across $5$ oscillatory bands (Delta $0.5\text{--}3.5\text{ Hz}$, Theta $3.8\text{--}8.5\text{ Hz}$, Beta $13\text{--}30\text{ Hz}$, Gamma $30\text{--}100\text{ Hz}$, and Dickey Ripples $70\text{--}100\text{ Hz}$), the engine computes the **Corrected Imaginary Phase-Locking Value ($ci\text{PLV}$)**:

$$ci\text{PLV}_{i, j, s} = \frac{\Im \left( \psi_{i, j, s} \right)}{\sqrt{1 - \left( \Re \left( \psi_{i, j, s} \right) \right)^2}} \in [-1.0, +1.0]$$

## 5. Continuous Stochasticity Continuum (`--stochastic 0.0 ... 1.0`) & Monty Modes

### 5.1 Unified Continuous Stochasticity Scale (`--stochastic`)
All servers, runners, and the Web UI operate on a single continuous parameter **`--stochastic` $s \in [0.0, 1.0]$**:
* **At $s = 1.0$ (Autonomous Baseline):** 100% standard, unperturbed generative synthesis (`MusicGen`, `LCM`, `LTX-Video`), where the model freely generates music or visuals from the base lore.
* **As $s \to 0.0$ (Deterministic Neurofeedback Mode):** Random sampling noise ($s \cdot g_{\text{rand}}$ in MusicGen; $\sin(s \frac{\pi}{2}) \mathbf{G}_{\text{rand}}$ in Diffusion) smoothly attenuates to zero while full-rank geodesic rotation of the continuous embeddings ($\theta_{\text{WM}} \propto 1 - s$) takes over 100% of the creative trajectory.

### 5.2 Monty Column Modes (`--monty-mode` / `--no-monty`)
* **`--monty-mode off` (`--no-monty`):** Bypasses Monty SDR quantization. Unassigned cortical regions without a physical EEG sensor remain anchored to their base lore concept (holding structural walls / rhythmic foundations), while regions with connected physical sensors are driven directly by raw working memory.
* **`--monty-mode missing_only` (Default):** Regions with a connected physical EEG device use 100% pure working memory, while Monty columns act as predictive operators (`L6a -> L1` top-down predictions and `L3 -> L4` upward anomalies) for regions without a physical sensor.
* **`--monty-mode full`:** Monty SDR columns are active across all 5 regions.

## 6. Multi-Modal Generative Engines (3D-DiT Video, 2D Diffusion, Zero-Seam MusicGen)

* **Zero-Seam Continuous MusicGen (`music_render_logic.py` & `musicgen_fast_kernels.py`):**
  * **Zero-Prefill Continuous KV-Cache:** Keeps the transformer's KV-cache open across chunks without re-encoding the 180-token history on every step, enabling blocks down to **0.02s (1 token = 20 ms)** and **0.10s (5 tokens)** at `> 2.0x RTF` on RTX 3060 and ultra-low latency on RTX 4090/5090.
  * **Full-Rank Embedding Injection:** Projects the full 64-mode SVD subspace (`max_bases=64`) into `[64 × 768]` Cross-Attention and injects the instantaneous 3000-D cortical slice (`[5 regions × 5 bands × 120 dipoles]`) into the 1024-D input embedding `input_` prior to Layer 1.
  * **Bitwise & Acoustic Seam Verification:** On startup, `run_startup_diagnostics` verifies 100% bitwise token identity between $12 \times 0.1\text{s}$ blocks and $1 \times 1.2\text{s}$ block and checks waveform continuity (`max_err < 0.03`) via two-sided `ExactStreamingEncodecDecoder` (`pad_tokens=24, lookahead_tokens=8`).
* **2D Diffusion (`render_logic.py`):** Preserves exact $\mathcal{N}(0, I)$ Gaussian statistics across all values of `--stochastic` via orthonormal $S^3$ basis rotation.
* **3D-DiT Video World (`video_heterarchy_composer.py`):** Evaluates Frobenius similarity across all regional slices $[S_r \times 4096]$ in 524,288-D T5-XXL space.

## 7. Lore-Agnostic Engine Policy & Universal Regional Lore

* **Zero Hardcoded Lore in Engine Code:** All Python engines (`music_render_logic.py`, `video_render_logic.py`, `render_logic.py`) are 100% lore-agnostic mathematical operators. All world, visual, and musical semantics live exclusively in external `.txt` files (`lore/`).
* **Universal Regional Lore Parser:** If a lore file (`lore/music_lore_psy.txt`, `lore/world_lore_psy.txt`, etc.) does not specify regional section headers (`[FPZ]`, `[AFZ]`, `[F3]`, `[F4]`, `[FCZ]`), its concepts are automatically assigned to **all 5 cortical regions**. If regional headers are specified, upper regions (`[FPZ]..[F4]`) hold the structural foundation while `[FCZ]` provides the active action manifold.
