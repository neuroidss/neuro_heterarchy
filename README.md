## 3. Hardware LSL Data Plane vs. Dynamic Cortical Control Plane (Multi-Board uHD EEG)

Grounded in ultra-high-density EEG (uHD EEG) neuroengineering (*Lee et al., Frontiers in Neuroscience 2022* [[23]](#ref-23)):

1. **Immortal Hardware LSL Streams (Data Plane):**
   Each physical `FreeEEG16-alpha2` board (26 mm diameter, 16 channels, 120 bipolar dipoles) is bound to a **permanent hardware LSL stream** named `FreeEEG_<ID>` (e.g., `FreeEEG_4A2B`). LSL outlets are **never** destroyed, renamed, or restarted when a board is moved across the head or reassigned to another user.
2. **Out-of-Band Cortical Routing & Auto-Return (Control Plane):**
   User identity (`User1..N`), cortical zone (`Fpz`, `AFz`, `F3`, `F4`, `FCz`, `Pz`, `Cz`, `Oz`, or `UNASSIGNED`), and PGA gain (`4x..32x`) are managed out-of-band via the Web/IPC Control Plane (`router.device_mappings`) and persisted in browser `localStorage`. If a board is rebooted or temporarily loses BLE connection, the browser automatically reconnects and restores its exact cortical assignment into the same LSL stream.
3. **Multi-Board uHD Spatial Fusion on a Single 10–20 Zone:**
   Multiple 26 mm `FreeEEG16-alpha2` pucks can be placed side-by-side within a single 10–20 zone (e.g., 3 boards on `FCz`). `DirectGPUSpectralFrontend` automatically fuses all boards assigned to the same zone via spatial phase-coherence weighting on the GPU.

## 4. Mathematical Foundations: Continuous Causal Demodulation & Bounded Geodesic Cones

### 4.1 Volume-Conduction-Free $ci\text{PLV} \in [-1.0, +1.0]$ & Carrier Cancellation (*Bruña et al., 2018; Miller et al., 2018*)
Following *Bruña, Maestú, & Pereda (2018)* ([DOI: 10.1088/1741-2552/aacfe4](https://doi.org/10.1088/1741-2552/aacfe4) [[10]](#ref-10), Eq. 14), for all $\binom{16}{2} = 120$ unique dipole pairs per board and $32$ stationary causal micro-windows $w_s(\tau)$ across $5$ oscillatory bands (Delta $0.8\text{--}3.5\text{ Hz}$, Theta $4.0\text{--}8.5\text{ Hz}$, Beta $13\text{--}30\text{ Hz}$, Gamma $30\text{--}100\text{ Hz}$, and Dickey Ripples $70\text{--}100\text{ Hz}$), the analytic cross-spectrum demodulates the carrier frequency $\omega_s t$:

$$\psi_{i, j, s}(t) = \sum_{\tau} w_s(\tau) \, P_{i,s}(t-\tau) \, P_{j,s}^*(t-\tau) = \sum_{\tau} w_s(\tau) \, e^{i\left(\Delta \phi_{i,j,s}(t-\tau)\right)}$$

$$ci\text{PLV}_{i, j, s}(t) = \frac{\Im \left( \psi_{i, j, s}(t) \right)}{\sqrt{1 - \left( \Re \left( \psi_{i, j, s}(t) \right) \right)^2}} \in [-1.0, +1.0]$$

Because the carrier phase $\omega_s t$ cancels out in $P_i P_j^*$ and the 32 causal Gaussian micro-windows $w_s(\tau)$ are stationary relative to the present moment $t$, the resulting $96,000\text{-D}$ tensor is completely free of artificial 1-second Delta/Theta carrier waves while updating continuously on every incoming 60 ms BLE packet via a multi-timescale synaptic calcium trace (*Miller, Lundqvist, & Bastos, Neuron 2018* [[4]](#ref-4)).

### 4.2 QR-Orthonormalized Geodesic Concept Cone (`universal_full_rank_manifold_step`)
To guarantee that 100% deterministic neurofeedback (`--stochastic 0.0`) remains strictly inside the semantic neighborhood of the target concept(s) without collapsing dimensionality:
1. **Coordinate System Invariance (`preserve_bos_token=True`):** Token `0` (`[BOS]` in CLIP) is preserved untouched so the global coordinate origin of U-Net never drifts.
2. **QR-Orthonormal Tangent Basis:** The semantic subspace spanned by the concept tokens $\mathbf{A}_{\text{sem}} \in \mathbb{R}^{(L-1) \times D}$ is orthonormalized via reduced QR decomposition ($\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{Q}^\top = \mathbf{I}$), preventing singular directions from amplifying noise.
3. **Bounded Geodesic Rotation (`wm_radius` $\in [0.0, 1.0]$):** The 160-slot working memory wave $\Delta\mathbf{W}$ is projected onto $\mathbf{Q}$, orthogonalized per token against $\mathbf{A}_{\text{sem}}$, and rotated along a great-circle geodesic arc bounded by the user-configurable radius $R_{\text{WM}} =$ `wm_radius`:

$$\theta_{\text{rad}} = R_{\text{WM}} \cdot \frac{\pi}{2.4} \cdot \tanh\!\left(25\,\|\Delta\mathbf{W}\|_2\right) (1 - s)$$

$$\mathbf{E}_{\text{out}} = \cos(\theta_{\text{rad}})\,\mathbf{A}_{\text{sem}} + \sin(\theta_{\text{rad}})\,\widehat{\mathbf{T}}_{\text{WM}}$$

## 5. Dual Stochasticity (`--stochastic` vs. `--unassigned-stochastic`), Concept Radius (`--wm-radius`) & Monty Modes

### 5.1 Dual Regional Stochasticity Continuum
NeuroCanvas separates stochasticity control between **active cortical regions** (regions with a connected physical EEG device or active Monty operator) and **unassigned cortical regions**:
* **`--stochastic` $s_{\text{act}} \in [0.0, 1.0]$ (Default `0.0`):** Controls the stochasticity of cortical regions where a user's EEG device is active.
  * At $s_{\text{act}} = 0.0$: **100% deterministic working-memory control** — every change in visual structure and musical phrasing is driven exclusively by the 96,000-D cortical field.
  * Raising $s_{\text{act}} \to 1.0$: Smoothly restores standard autonomous stochastic generation across all 4 latent channels and all 4 EnCodec codebooks, even when only a single EEG device (e.g., on `FCz`) is connected.
* **`--unassigned-stochastic` $s_{\text{unass}} \in [0.0, 1.0]$ (Default `1.0`):** Controls the stochasticity of unassigned cortical regions when `--monty-mode off` is selected.
  * At $s_{\text{unass}} = 1.0$: Unassigned background regions maintain lively, natural generative dynamics.
  * At $s_{\text{unass}} = 0.0$ (with $s_{\text{act}} = 0.0$): **Strict 100% Bitwise Determinism Mode for Scientific Experiments** — zero random number generation across the entire pipeline; identical EEG input produces bitwise-identical video and audio SHA-256 hashes without requiring multi-trial statistical averaging.

### 5.2 Concept Region Radius (`--wm-radius` $\in [0.02, 0.80]$)
Available live in the WebUI (`🧭 Радиус области концепта`) and CLI (`--wm-radius`):
* Controls both the maximum geodesic angle $\theta_{\text{rad}}$ in CLIP/T5 embedding space and the 3-angle Euler–Hopf rotation amplitude ($\phi_a, \phi_b, \phi_c$) on the $S^3$ Gaussian latent sphere.
* Small values (`0.05 – 0.15`) lock generation tightly inside the core of the concept; larger values (`0.30 – 0.80`) unlock wide, high-variance deterministic exploration driven by all 96,000 modes of working memory at zero stochasticity.
