## 1. Core Paradigm: Intransitive Mutable Catalysts & Zero-Imba RPG Party-Building

Conventional game engines and AI simulators suffer from two opposite failure modes:
1. **Static Hierarchy ("Imba" Dominance):** One dominant entity or rule unilaterally defeats or modifies others while remaining permanently immune itself ($A > B > C$).
2. **Symmetric Chaos ("Brown Mud" Blending):** Every entity simultaneously modifies every other entity on the same frame, causing 3D-DiT Cross-Attention (`attn2`) to collapse into an unstructured texture soup.

**Exocortex 3D-DiT** solves this by synthesizing **Warren McCulloch's Neural Heterarchy** (*McCulloch, 1945* [[1]](#ref-1)), **Stuart Kauffman's Reflexively Autocatalytic Sets** (*Kauffman, 1986* [[8]](#ref-8)), and **Winfree's DNA Strand Displacement Cascades** (*Soloveichik et al., 2010* [[9]](#ref-9)) into a single universal primitive: the **Mutable Working Memory Catalyst (`WM-Agent`)**.

### The Law of Mutable Catalysts (RPG Party-Building Ontology):
1. **Local Catalytic Invariance (Asymmetry Within a Single Reaction):**
   In any active reaction $e_{\text{cat}}(s_c) + e_{\text{sub}}(s_a) \longrightarrow e_{\text{cat}}(s_c) + e_{\text{sub}}(s_b)$, the **Catalyst** ($e_{\text{cat}}$) **is not consumed and is not mutated back by the substrate** (`INV: 100% [CAT:UNCONSUMED]`). It acts as an unyielding morphogenetic field that drives the **Substrate** ($e_{\text{sub}}$) across a phase transition $s_a \to s_b$.
2. **Global Intransitive Mutability (Zero "Imba" Across the Ring):**
   No entity is an invincible "imba". Every catalyst that is invariant in Reaction $k$ **becomes the mutable substrate in Reaction $m$** when acted upon by its own upstream counter-catalyst:


* **`CAT(e2 -> e3)`:** `e2` (`F3`) is the unconsumed catalyst (`INV:100%`); `e3` (`F4`) is the mutable substrate (`s3 -> s4/s5`).
* **`CAT(e3 -> e0)`:** `e3` (`F4`) becomes the unconsumed catalyst (`INV:100%`); `e0` (`FCz`) becomes the mutable substrate (`s0 -> s1/s2`).
* **`CAT(e0 -> e1)`:** `e0` (`FCz`) becomes the unconsumed catalyst (`INV:100%`); `e1` (`AFz`) becomes the mutable substrate (`s0 -> s1/s2`).

Because every entity has both **targets it can catalyze** and **predators that can catalyze it**, building a party of `WM-Agents` (`allowed_pool` configurations bound to cortical regions or `FreeEEG16` boards) becomes a genuine strategic deck-building / RPG synergetic system where every state transition alters the balance of power in the 128D latent world.

## 7. Next Session Roadmap: Eliminating Inter-Stage Visual Jumps via Multi-Frame Temporal Context

### Diagnosis of Current Visual Jumpiness (Observed in the 56-Block Run):
In the latest 112-second (56-block) run, all 524,288-D choices and 128D branch transitions (`Stage 1 -> Stage 4 -> Stage 1 -> Stage 3 -> Stage 4`) worked with 100% mathematical stability, **but visual transitions occasionally exhibited abrupt jumps when the active pair or winning concept switched between blocks**:
1. **Single-Latent-Frame Bottleneck (`cond_frames = 1` out of `total_lat_frames = 5`):**
   Currently, `last_clean_latent_1frame` freezes only **1 latent frame** (`[:, :, :1]`), which carries **zero velocity/momentum derivative** ($\partial \mathbf{Z} / \partial t = 0$) across the boundary of a block.
2. **Within-Stage Catalyst Mode Flipping:**
   Because `select_concept_full_rank_524288d` re-evaluates `top_cat_id` on every single 2-second block even *mid-reaction* (`maneuver_phase > 0`), the catalyst's visual sentence in `scene_prompt` can flip mid-stream (e.g., `e2_s2` Hexagonal Bastion on Block #2 $\to$ `e2_s1` Amethyst Mirror on Block #3), forcing the 4 unconditioned latent frames to abruptly re-sculpt the scene.
3. **2-Entity Prompt Exclusion on Branch Switch:**
   When a reaction commits at $100\%$ and switches from `CAT(e2->e3)` to `CAT(e0->e1)`, the prompt abruptly drops `e2, e3` and inserts `e0, e1` with only 1 latent frame of overlap.

### Engineering Plan for Next Session:
1. **2-Latent-Frame Velocity-Preserving Conditioning Window (`cond_lat_frames = 2` of `total_lat_frames = 6`) + Soft Decay Mask:**
   Pass the **last 2 clean latent frames** (`unpacked_z0[:, :, -2:]`) into the next window with a graded conditioning mask (`mask[:, :, 0] = 1.0`, `mask[:, :, 1] = 0.65`) so 3D-DiT preserves both **position ($\mathbf{Z}$)** and **temporal momentum ($\partial\mathbf{Z}/\partial t$)** across block boundaries!
2. **Intra-Reaction Choice Hysteresis (Latching Chosen `(Catalyst_Mode, Target_Mode)` Until Reaction Commit):**
   Allow the 524,288-D Working Memory to continuously update the probability distribution `*WIN / oALT` on every block, but **latch the chosen `(start_id, target_id, cat_id)` sentence pair during `maneuver_phase > 0`** (or require a strong hysteresis margin $>25\%$ to flip mid-reaction), while applying smooth 524,288-D geodesic rotation on every block.
3. **Persistent World Footprint Tokens:**
   Keep a compact 10-token visual footprint of the previously crystallized state in `scene_prompt` during the first block of a new branch so the old structure visibly morphs into the new catalytic pair rather than cutting away.
