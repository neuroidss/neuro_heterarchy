## 1. Core Paradigm: Intransitive Mutable Catalysts & Zero-Imba RPG Party-Building

## 5. RTX 3060 (12GB) Fast-Stream Optimizations & Smooth Realtime Video Pacer (`RTF` Meter)

To achieve continuous, non-jerky world generation on a single consumer **RTX 3060 (12GB VRAM)**:

1. **Native `bfloat16` Transformer Execution (No Slow Software `float8` Casting on 12GB GPUs):**
   On Ampere GPUs (RTX 3060), software `enable_layerwise_casting(torch.float8_e4m3fn)` incurs a ~35% per-layer dequantization penalty. Since the entire LTX-Video pipeline (4-bit NF4 T5-XXL + native `bfloat16` 3D-DiT + 3D-VAE) fits in **~7.3 GB VRAM**, `neuro_ltx_backend.py` keeps the 3D-DiT in native `bfloat16` on all GPUs with $\ge 9.5\text{ GB}$ VRAM.
2. **Startup T5 Pair Pre-Warming (`_warmup_manifest_semantics` = 0.0 ms T5 in Loop):**
   All individual archetype probes and all $(K_{\text{cat}} \times K_{\text{sub}})$ scene combinations are encoded once at startup and cached in VRAM. During live generation, T5 encoding takes **0.0 ms**.
3. **Minimal Latent Step (`--step-latents 1` = 8 Frames / 0.5s) + Selective CFG:**
   * Each step generates **1 new latent frame** (`8` RGB frames = `0.5s` of video) conditioned on `--context-latents` (`1` or `2` latent frames).
   * **Selective CFG (`neuro_solver_stepper.py`):** Classifier-Free Guidance (`batch=2`, `cfg=2.6`) is executed only on the first half of the denoising steps (e.g., steps 1–2 of 4) to establish sharp crystal/nebula structure and reject brown mud, while the remaining steps execute with `batch=1` (`pos_emb` only), cutting transformer compute by **40%**.
   * **Inter-Block Latent & Optical Cross-Fade:** The boundary between the context tail and the new latent frame is smoothly blended in latent space (`inertia_blend`) and across the first 3 RGB frames in `SmoothRealtimeVideoPacer`, eliminating visible block seams.
4. **Smooth Realtime Video Pacer (`SmoothRealtimeVideoPacer` in `neuro_run.py`):**
   Modeled after the adaptive resampler in `music_render_logic.py`:
   * Continuously measures the **Realtime Factor (`RTF = raw_video_ms / gen_ms`)**.
   * If `RTF < 1.0` (e.g., `0.5s` of video generated in `0.75s` $\to$ `RTF = 0.67x`), the pacer automatically stretches playback (`play_speed = 0.67x`) via smooth linear frame interpolation (`cv2.addWeighted`) so the visual stream flows at a constant **16 FPS with zero pauses or freezes**.
   * If `RTF >= 1.0`, the HUD and terminal explicitly display the percentage of **spare compute headroom** (e.g., `+20% ZAPAS -> OK TO ADD --context-latents 2`), telling you when you can safely increase `--context-latents` or `--steps`.

## 6. Toggleable Diagnostic HUD (`--hud off|minimal|full` & Hotkey `[H]`)

You can control or hide the diagnostic overlay both via CLI flags and live during a run:
* **`--hud off` (or `--no-hud`):** **Default for Neurofeedback.** Renders a 100% clean, unannotated generative world in the Pygame window, browser stream (`:8080`), and saved `.mp4`, while writing full telemetry to the terminal and `_telemetry.jsonl`.
* **`--hud minimal`:** Displays only a thin top status bar showing `RTF`, `PlaySpd`, `Lag (ms)` / `Headroom (%)`, `Gen (ms)`, `dS%`, and Feigenbaum `r`.
* **`--hud full`:** Displays the full 4-agent choice cards (`*WIN` vs. `oALT`), bottom trajectory graph, and 128D branch competition, with all percentages smoothly interpolated at 60 FPS across frames.
* **Live Hotkey `[H]` in the Pygame Window:** Cycles `OFF` $\to$ `MINIMAL` $\to$ `FULL` $\to$ `OFF` on the fly without interrupting generation.
